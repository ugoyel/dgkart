Delete your DKKart account

In the app: open My DKKart → Delete account and confirm. Your account is removed immediately.

Without the app: email [SUPPORT EMAIL] from any address with the subject "Delete my account" and your registered mobile number. We verify ownership by OTP and delete within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your profile (mobile number, name, email), saved addresses, cart and watchlist.

What is kept

Invoices and order records are kept for up to 8 years as required by Indian tax law, and are no longer linked to a profile.
