This policy explains how [LEGAL BUSINESS NAME] ("DKKart", "DGkart.com", "we") collects, uses and protects your information when you use the DKKart mobile app and website.

Information we collect

  • Mobile number: to create your account and sign you in with a one-time password (OTP). OTP SMS delivery is handled by Google Firebase Authentication.
  • Profile details you choose to add: name and email address.
  • Delivery addresses: name, phone, address and PIN code, used to deliver your orders.
  • Orders, cart and watchlist: items you view, save and buy.
  • Payment information: payments are processed by Razorpay. We never see or store your full card, UPI PIN or bank credentials; we receive a payment reference and status only.
  • Technical data: device type, app version and logs needed to keep the service secure and working.

How we use it

  • To create and secure your account, process orders and payments, and deliver purchases.
  • To provide customer support and send order-related messages.
  • To prevent fraud and comply with legal obligations (including tax and accounting rules).

We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing

We share data only with service providers that help us run DKKart: Google Firebase (OTP sign-in), Razorpay (payments), our hosting and storage providers, and delivery partners (shipping address and phone only). They may use it only to provide their service to us.

Retention and deletion

We keep account data while your account is active and order records as long as the law requires. You can ask us to delete your account and personal data at any time by writing to [SUPPORT EMAIL]; we will confirm within 30 days.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access is restricted to authorised staff.

Children

DKKart is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data.
